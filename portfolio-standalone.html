My Vision

Why Barcelona?

Barcelona is one of the world's most creative cities — a place where Gaudí's surrealism shares streets with cutting-edge design studios and global brand headquarters. Studying here means immersing myself in a culture that treats creativity not as decoration, but as infrastructure.

I chose this programme because it bridges what I've always kept separate: the analytical rigour of strategy and the intuitive freedom of creativity. I want to build brands that don't just sell, but resonate — brands that people genuinely belong to.

In five years, I see myself leading creative strategy at a European brand consultancy or launching my own studio focused on purpose-driven brands in Southern Europe and beyond.
